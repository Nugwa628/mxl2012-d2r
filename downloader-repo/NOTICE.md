## What the downloader installs

The downloader installs D2R-XL 2012, a fan port of **Median XL 2012** by Brother Laz to Diablo II: Resurrected. The mod's
files are downloaded from the D2R-XL 2012 download site and are not part of this repository; Median XL 2012's content
belongs to its author. **Diablo II** and **Diablo II: Resurrected** belong to Blizzard Entertainment: no game files are in
this repository. **D2RLoader** (<https://d2rloader.net>) is a separate program with its own license; the downloader fetches
it from its official site only when you confirm Install D2RLoader. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by
Blizzard Entertainment or Median XL's author.
