# The developer menu

A cheat and testing menu built into D2R-XL 2012. It works in **single player only**; in a TCP/IP game or a game you joined it stays closed for
actions and says why.

Press **Ctrl+F12** in a game to open or close it (change the key under Options > Controls, "Developer Menu").

## Tabs

- **Items:** click the box, type part of a name and pick a category (Uniques, Sets, Charms, Quest / misc, Bases, Runewords). The arrow next to a row
  gives you that item in your backpack, or in your Horadric Cube or stash if the backpack cannot take it. If it fits nowhere, the menu says so and
  no item is made. Normal weapons and armour come with their maximum number of empty sockets ("Socketed (N)" in the tooltip: put gems, runes
  or jewels in as usual); uniques and set items come as they drop (their own sockets, or none).
- **Cornerstone of the World** has one row per skill: search "cornerstone", the skill ("ghost arrow") or the class ("cornerstone amazon").
- **Typing:** while the box has the text cursor, every key types into it, including keys that are game hotkeys (letters, digits). Click outside
  the box (anywhere else on the menu) and the hotkeys work again.
- **Runewords** give you a *kit*: the base item with the right sockets and the runes. Socket the runes yourself, in the order the row lists.
- **Travel:** type part of an area's name, and the arrow takes you there. Areas marked "map only, the fight needs its normal summon" are the uber
  arenas: you can look at the map, but the fight still needs its normal summon.
  Uber levels come first, under **Uber levels**: type an uber level's name (or "uber" for all 16), and its arrow takes you to the area right
  before it, shown in brackets (for Fauztinville, Stony Tomb Level 1). Its entrance, or its red portal for Toraja, Old Bremmtown and Khalimgrad,
  is somewhere in that area; you arrive where the game puts anyone entering that area, so walk to the entrance from there.
- **Character:** type a level in the box and press **Raise to level**. It only goes up, and you get the normal stat and skill points.
- **Unlocks:** opens every waypoint on Normal, Nightmare and Hell, and the act tabs of the waypoint panel.
- **Quests:** "Advanced: complete quests", then **Confirm** (rewards are not guaranteed, as the line under the button says). It uses the mod loader's cheat for the current difficulty. Skill points,
  stat points, Akara's and Larzuk's services and the like may not all be given. Use it on test characters.

Everything here changes your character like normal play would and is saved the normal way: there is no undo. Items you make can go into the shared
stash like any other.
