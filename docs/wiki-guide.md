# The in-game wiki

D2R-XL 2012 carries its own reference inside the game: uniques, sets, runewords, gemwords, base items, cube recipes, monsters, skills,
ubers and the guide pages. It works offline, in single player and in multiplayer, and it changes nothing in your game.

Press **J** in a game to open or close it (change the key under Options > Controls, "Wiki"). It needs HD graphics: in classic graphics the
key only says so.

- **Search:** click the box at the top and type. Every word you type has to be in the entry, in any order: an item's name, its base, or
  one of its stats ("cold spell damage"). While the box has the text cursor every key types into it, also keys that are game hotkeys;
  click anywhere else on the panel and the hotkeys work again.
- **Categories:** the row of buttons shows one kind of entry; **All** shows everything. A search stays when you change the category.
- **The list:** the arrow in front of a row opens that entry on the right. The arrows under the list, or the mouse wheel over the list,
  turn its pages.
- **The text:** the mouse wheel over it, the arrows under it, or Page Up / Page Down scroll it. When you open an entry from a search, the
  text starts at the first line with your words, and every line with them has a `>` in front.
- **Links:** some entries end with "See also". The arrow in front of a line there opens that entry; **Back** returns.
- **Close:** J again, Esc, or the cross.

With nothing typed, the wiki opens on its Welcome page.
