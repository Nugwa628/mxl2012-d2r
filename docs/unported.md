# Unported, approximated and different from Median XL 2012

Rule: the port follows Median XL 2012 v005, not D2R, wherever the two differ. Anything that cannot be done
yet, or is left out on purpose, gets a row here with the reason. Each lane appends its gaps.

Status: **open** (not ported yet), **approx** (works, not exactly as Median), **deviation** (deliberately different,
with the reason), **dropped** (not part of v005 or not worth porting, with the reason), **verify** (ported, Median
behaviour not yet confirmed in game).
